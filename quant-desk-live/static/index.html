This is an analysis tool. It cannot trade and never will. It reads Bybit's public market data and nothing else — no account, no API key, no credentials of any kind, and no code path anywhere in it that can place, modify or cancel an order. "Tracker Positions" are simulated arithmetic against live prices, not orders. Not investment advice. Prices stream live from Bybit's public market data feed; on the default perpetuals setting the funding, open-interest, liquidation and long/short figures are public derivatives data, read-only. "Likelihood" and "Direction" are a qualitative heuristic (momentum, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic, moving averages, 52-week proximity, support/resistance, multi-timeframe confluence, and RSI/price divergence), not a statistical probability or a recommendation. The Signal Track Record panel is a real historical backtest of this watchlist's own price data, now reported net of trading costs and against the unconditional base rate — but past performance of a signal is not a guarantee of future results, sample sizes (shown as "n=") are often small, and because the watchlist is populated with pairs selected for already having moved, its numbers carry a selection bias that flatters them. The Signal Scorecard is the more trustworthy of the two: it records what the desk said before each outcome existed, so it has no such bias — it just needs time and closed positions to become meaningful. Positioning data (funding, open interest, liquidations, long/short ratio) describes how the market is positioned, not when it will move; crowded can stay crowded for a long time. Spotlight's headline read is the same kind of technical-analysis heuristic, not a prediction — it states what a specific set of indicators across 1m-12h currently imply and flags when they're genuinely uncertain (an RSI extreme or divergence), but markets can and do move against any reading, and low-timeframe leveraged trading carries real, fast risk of loss. Verify anything here before acting on it, and size any position with that risk in mind.
